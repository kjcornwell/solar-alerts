Features

Built around the sun.

Set alerts your way

  • Before, at or after sunset or sunrise: one tap for 10 minutes to 2 hours, or your own hours and minutes (up to 999 minutes)
  • Every day, weekdays, weekends, days you pick, or just once
  • A sun timeline and a live notification preview show exactly when and how it goes off
  • Every setting on one page, or Guided mode: one question at a time
  • Optional labels like "Evening walk" or "Photography time"
  • Notifications count down live; snooze right from the notification
  • Precise alarms that fire on time, even when your phone is idle

Know the sun

  • Live countdown to the next sunset or sunrise
  • Today's and tomorrow's sunrise and sunset, to the second
  • Day length and how it compares to yesterday
  • Solar noon, golden hour, blue hour, and civil, nautical and astronomical twilight
  • Handles midnight sun and polar night in the far north and south

Solar events & celestial calendar

  • Solar Clock and timeline views of where the sun is right now
  • Browse every solar event for any date
  • Upcoming celestial events and meteor showers
  • Verified dates for eclipses, meteor shower peaks, planet oppositions, equinoxes and solstices through 2031
  • Moon phase and the next full and new moon

Also: six themes (Solar Classic, Kodachrome ’70s, Ukiyo-e Woodblock, Mission Control, Observatory Brass and Riso Almanac), each with a day and a night look, plus countdown card layouts from Sun Arc to Departure Board that you can preview live before saving. And 12-hour or 24-hour time, share today's sunset time with friends, and built-in help for every setting.

Privacy

Wherever you are. Privately.

Sun times are calculated on your device. Your location is used for that and nothing else.

  • On-deviceYour location never leaves your phone.
  • No accountsNothing to sign up for, nothing to log in to.
  • No ads, no trackingNo advertising ID, no analytics SDKs.
  • Uses your GPS location, or enter a place manually
  • Follow Me mode keeps times accurate as you travel
  • Adjusts automatically for daylight saving time

Release notes

What's new in 1.2.76

1.2.76

LatestNovember 2, 2026
  • Themes: six looks for the whole app, each with a day and a night version, plus new countdown card layouts. Tap the palette icon on Home to flip through them live, then Save.
  • New alert editor: every setting on one page, or Guided mode with one question at a time. A sun timeline shows your alert against sunrise or sunset, and a preview shows exactly what the notification will say.
  • Quick times from 10 minutes to 2 hours, or set your own hours and minutes.
  • Live countdown in alerts: notifications count down to sunrise or sunset second by second.
  • Pausing or deleting an alert now clears anything it already had scheduled, and early-morning alerts set many hours before sunrise go off on the right evening.

1.2.59

October 31, 2026
  • The Solar Clock marks sunrise and sunset, with time labels every 3 hours; tap it for details.
  • Home shows today's date, the time and how much daylight you gained or lost since yesterday.
  • Full-screen location search with suggestions.
  • Countdown no longer an hour off on clock-change nights.

1.2.57

October 28, 2026
  • Far north and far south: during midnight sun or polar night, the app now says there is no sunrise or sunset today instead of showing times from weeks away, and no longer schedules alerts from the wrong day.
  • Equinox and solstice times for years beyond the built-in calendar are now accurate to about a minute and shown in your time zone.

Help Solar Alerts launch.

Google needs at least 12 people to test a new app for 14 days before it can go public. If you have an Android phone, it takes two minutes:

  1. Join the tester group. Open groups.google.com/g/solar-alerts-testers with your Google account and tap Join group.
  2. Opt in to the test. Open the Solar Alerts test page and tap Become a tester.
  3. Install and use it. Get Solar Alerts from Google Play, set an alert or two, and keep it installed for 14 days.
