Features

Built around the sun.

Set alerts your way

  • Any number of minutes before or after sunset or sunrise
  • Daily or one-time alerts, with day-of-week selection
  • Optional labels like "Evening walk" or "Photography time"
  • Snooze right from the notification
  • Precise alarms that fire on time, even when your phone is idle

Know the sun

  • Live countdown to the next sunset or sunrise
  • Today's and tomorrow's sunrise and sunset, to the second
  • Day length and how it compares to yesterday
  • Solar noon, golden hour, blue hour, and civil, nautical and astronomical twilight
  • Handles midnight sun and polar night in the far north and south

Solar events & celestial calendar

  • Solar Clock and timeline views of where the sun is right now
  • Browse every solar event for any date
  • Upcoming celestial events and meteor showers
  • Verified dates for eclipses, meteor shower peaks, planet oppositions, equinoxes and solstices through 2031
  • Moon phase and the next full and new moon

Also: 12-hour or 24-hour time, light and dark themes, share today's sunset time with friends, and built-in help for every setting.

Privacy

Wherever you are. Privately.

Sun times are calculated on your device. Your location is used for that and nothing else.

  • On-deviceYour location never leaves your phone.
  • No accountsNothing to sign up for, nothing to log in to.
  • No ads, no trackingNo advertising ID, no analytics SDKs.
  • Uses your GPS location, or enter a place manually
  • Follow Me mode keeps times accurate as you travel
  • Adjusts automatically for daylight saving time

What's new in 1.2.57

1.2.57

LatestOctober 28, 2026
  • Far north and far south: during midnight sun or polar night, the app now says there is no sunrise or sunset today instead of showing times from weeks away, and no longer schedules alerts from the wrong day.

October 28, 2026

1.2.55

October 27, 2026
  • Day-of-week alerts no longer fire on the other days.
  • Repeating alerts re-arm the moment they fire, so the next day's alert is never skipped.
  • Alerts reschedule automatically after an update or a time zone change.
  • Smaller download.
