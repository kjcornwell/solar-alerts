Solar Alerts Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026 · Developer: 3Root (Kevin Cornwell)

Summary: Solar Alerts calculates sunrise, sunset, golden hour and related times on your device. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, sell or share your personal data.

Location

The app uses your device's precise or approximate location (with your permission) to calculate solar event times and schedule your alerts. Location is processed on your device and stored only in the app's local storage. It is never sent to the developer.

To get your location and to look up place names, the app uses Android system services: Google Play services location and the Android Geocoder. Those services are operated by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. You can also enter a location manually instead of granting location permission.

Notifications and alarms

Notification and alarm permissions are used only to deliver the alerts you configure. Alert settings stay on your device.

Feedback and diagnostic logs (optional)

If you choose to send feedback from the app, it opens your email app with a pre-filled message containing the app version, device model and Android version. You can choose to attach the app's diagnostic log, which may include app events and location coordinates used for calculations. Nothing is sent unless you press send in your email app. We use these emails only to respond to you and fix problems, and delete them when no longer needed.

Sharing

If you use the Share feature, the app hands the text you chose to share (for example, sun times for a location) to the app you pick. Nothing is shared automatically.

Data we do not collect

  • No account, name, email, or contacts
  • No advertising ID, ads, or analytics SDKs
  • No data sold or shared with third parties

Data retention and deletion

All app data lives on your device. Clear the app's data in Android Settings or uninstall the app to delete it. To ask us to delete any feedback email you sent, contact us at the address below.

Children

Solar Alerts is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
